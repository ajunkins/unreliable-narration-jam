INTRODUCTION:
The parking lot for the grocery store is completely empty, but the inside is a mess. Smashed and rotten produce on the floor. Tangles of shopping carts in the aisles. Whole shelving units knocked into one another like dominoes. 

It must have been hit early.

You're used to a din of voices over soft music, the beeping of registers, the squeaking wheels on mistreated shopping carts. The utter silence is unnerving, but the guidebook always says to push past your discomfort to survive. The people here were panicked. With the guidebook in hand, you'll make no such mistakes. 

You make your way away from the stench of the produce aisle.
